XPChores — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Overview

XPChores ("the App") is a family chore management app built with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app data stays on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers for advertising
  • Any user-generated content

Local Storage

The App stores your data (kid profiles, chore lists, XP progress, and settings) locally on your device using standard device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional premium content through in-app subscriptions. All purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store any payment information. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

XPChores is designed for families and may be used by children under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any users. All data remains on the device under the parent's control.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
