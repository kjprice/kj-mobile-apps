Two Sparks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Overview

Two Sparks ("the App") is a couples question app built with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app data stays on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers for advertising
  • Any user-generated content

Local Storage

The App stores your preferences (such as streak count, favorites, and settings) locally on your device using standard device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional premium content through in-app purchases. All purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store any payment information. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 17. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at twosparksapp@gmail.com.
