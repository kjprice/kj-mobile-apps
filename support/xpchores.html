Common Questions

How do I add a new kid?

Go to the Home tab and tap the "+" button to create a new kid profile with a name, avatar, and color.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases", or visit the Premium Upgrade screen and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

Is my data backed up?

XPChores stores all data locally on your device. Data is not synced to the cloud. We recommend keeping your device backed up via iCloud or Google.
