Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I see the same question as yesterday?

Each question is tied to the calendar date — everyone sees the same question on the same day. A new question unlocks at midnight in your local timezone.

I lost my streak — can it be restored?

Streaks reset if you miss a calendar day. Unfortunately we can't restore streaks manually since all data is stored locally on your device.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Your purchase is tied to your App Store / Google Play account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iOS: Settings app → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Two Sparks.
On Android: Google Play Store → Subscriptions → Two Sparks.

Is my data private?

Yes. Two Sparks stores everything locally on your device. We collect no personal data and have no servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
